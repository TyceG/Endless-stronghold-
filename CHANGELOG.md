# Changelog

## 0.7.0

- Generator settings: `hub_y` and `lowest_y` in the world preset.
- New icon; README with a map of the stronghold.
- A benchmark server: the stronghold generates about five times faster than a normal Overworld.

## 0.6.0

- Eyes of ender and `/locate structure endless_stronghold:portal_room` find the nearest portal room.

## 0.5.1

- Chests use vanilla's stronghold loot tables directly; no extra loot.

## 0.5.0

- The stronghold grows the way vanilla grows one, with vanilla's algorithm, region by region, joined by main corridors so it never ends. A portal room in every region.

## 0.4.0

- Straight cobblestone staircases; five-way crossings only where their doorways lead somewhere.

## 0.3.0

- Every room is vanilla's piece, block for block; floors 12 apart for vanilla's staircases.

## 0.2.0

- Four floors, region mazes, the room catalog, doors, and chests with loot.

## 0.1.0

- The world type, a walkable grid of halls on one floor, and the spawn room.
